Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

At BenchmarkResult - HF Plays, we believe in open benchmarks and total transparency. We build tools to help you evaluate gaming and device performance without compromising your digital privacy. Here is a clear, no-nonsense look at how we handle your data.

What We Collect (And What We Don't)

Explore all you want—no account or login is required. We do not ask for sensitive personal details. Information we directly receive is what you voluntarily send us through our contact form, such as your name, email, or benchmark request. Advertising partners may process technical information as described below.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use lightweight browser storage to remember your theme, view preference, and privacy notice state. You can clear this cache at any time via your browser settings.

Advertising Choices

Optional advertising cookies are enabled only after you choose to accept them. You can reject them when the privacy notice appears or reopen Privacy choices from the footer to change your decision. Google AdSense and its certified consent technology may process information according to your choice and their applicable policies.

How Your Information Is Used

Any feedback, test suggestions, or messages submitted are used exclusively to respond to your inquiries and refine our benchmark data. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

External Links & Third Parties

Our website loads icons via Cloudflare (Font Awesome), may display Google AdSense advertising when advertising is enabled, and features direct links to our official HF Plays community channels across YouTube, TikTok, Discord, Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Advertising partners may use cookies, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. Google explains its use of data on How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites. Please review the privacy terms of external platforms when interacting with them.

Data Retention & Security

We retain submitted contact records only for as long as necessary to address your feedback or support needs. We use industry-standard safeguards to keep your messages protected at all times.

Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your data. If you wish to review, update, or permanently delete any messages or information you previously submitted, reach out to us directly through the contact page.

Policy Updates

As we add new performance tools and testing features, this policy may evolve. Any updates will always be posted here with the revision date clearly stated above.
