Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

At BenchmarkResult - HF Plays, we believe in open benchmarks and total transparency. We build tools to help you evaluate gaming and device performance without compromising your digital privacy. Here is a clear, no-nonsense look at how we handle your data.

What We Collect (And What We Don't)

Explore all you want—no account or login is required. We do not track sensitive personal details. The only personal data we ever receive is what you voluntarily send us through our contact form (such as your name, email, or benchmark request).

Cookies & Local Storage

We keep tracking to a bare minimum. We use lightweight, essential browser storage solely to remember your cookie preferences and avoid annoying popups on every visit. You can clear this cache at any time via your browser settings.

How Your Information Is Used

Any feedback, test suggestions, or messages submitted are used exclusively to respond to your inquiries and refine our benchmark data. We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

External Links & Third Parties

Our website loads essential icons via Cloudflare (Font Awesome) and features direct links to our official HF Plays community channels across YouTube, TikTok, Discord, Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn. Please review their respective privacy terms when interacting with those external platforms.

Data Retention & Security

We retain submitted contact records only for as long as necessary to address your feedback or support needs. We use industry-standard safeguards to keep your messages protected at all times.

Your Rights & Choices

You have full control over your data. If you wish to review, update, or permanently delete any messages or information you previously submitted, reach out to us directly through the Contacts page.

Policy Updates

As we add new performance tools and testing features, this policy may evolve. Any updates will always be posted here with the revision date clearly stated above.
