About BenchmarkResult

An independent gaming performance archive by HF Plays, founded and maintained by Hilmi Fauzan

BenchmarkResult is a public archive of real-world gaming tests published by HF Plays. It helps viewers understand what a specific laptop, desktop, or integrated GPU can realistically deliver before changing settings or making a hardware decision.

What We Publish

Each entry connects a game with the device, resolution, graphics preset, Average FPS, 1% Low FPS, temperature readings, test date, and available video evidence. The archive is organized by mobile and desktop hardware so readers can move from a device overview to a specific game result.

Who Maintains It

HF Plays is the independent gaming project founded and maintained by Hilmi Fauzan. BenchmarkResult is the project’s public benchmark archive. Games are tested through a hands-on workflow using HDMI capture, a separate recording device, and an on-screen performance overlay. Results are reviewed and added to the archive after the related video is prepared for publication.

Why the Archive Exists

Average FPS alone does not tell the whole story. By preserving 1% Low FPS, temperature readings, settings, and practical conclusions, the archive gives viewers more context for deciding whether a game is playable and which settings are worth trying.

How to Use the Results

Start with the Mobile or Desktop device list, open a device, and choose a game. Read Average FPS together with 1% Low: a large gap can indicate dips or an inconsistent experience. Compare results only when the resolution, preset, driver, power mode, and memory configuration are similar.
